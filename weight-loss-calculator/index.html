Short answer. In the main trials, the average adult without diabetes lost 14.9% of body weight on Wegovy over 68 weeks and 15.0 to 20.9% on Zepbound or Mounjaro over 72 weeks.1,3 People with type 2 diabetes lost less (7.0 to 9.6%).2 Studies put anywhere from about 15% to 40 to 60% of that loss as lean mass, and protein plus resistance training push it toward the low end.5

How this calculator works

The calculator applies one number to your weight: the average percent change in the treatment arm of each drug's main trial. STEP 1 tested semaglutide 2.4 mg (Wegovy) in 1,961 adults without diabetes for 68 weeks.1 STEP 2 tested semaglutide 1 mg and 2.4 mg in adults with type 2 diabetes for 68 weeks.2 SURMOUNT-1 tested tirzepatide 5, 10 and 15 mg (Zepbound, Mounjaro) in adults without diabetes for 72 weeks.3 STEP UP tested the higher 7.2 mg semaglutide dose against 2.4 mg and placebo in adults with obesity and without diabetes for 72 weeks: 18.7 percent average loss on 7.2 mg, 15.6 percent on 2.4 mg and 3.9 percent on placebo.8 The retatrutide figure comes from a 48-week phase 2 trial of a drug that is not yet approved.4

The placebo figure matters as much as the drug figure. Every arm in these trials, placebo included, received diet and physical activity counseling. Therefore the gap between the two numbers is the drug's effect, and the placebo number is what the lifestyle advice alone produced.

The muscle estimate uses the same model as the site's other calculators. Reviews of the trial data put lean mass (everything that is not fat, mostly muscle plus water and organs) at anywhere from about 15% to 40 to 60% of GLP-1 weight loss, depending on the study.5 The tirzepatide baseline is 26%, the share measured in the SURMOUNT-1 body-composition substudy.5 The best case applies a resistance-training effect measured in older adults who were dieting without a GLP-1, so it is shown as a range rather than a single number.6

Honest limits

Three limits apply. First, an average hides a wide spread: in STEP 1 about half of participants lost 15% or more, and some lost little.1 Second, the trials ran 48 to 72 weeks, so the figures say nothing about year three or what happens after stopping (most people regain about two thirds of the loss within a year of stopping semaglutide).7 Third, trial participants had close follow-up and counseling that everyday care may not match. Use this as a starting point for a conversation with your prescriber, not as a target.

Frequently asked

How much weight do people lose on Wegovy?

In STEP 1, adults without diabetes on semaglutide 2.4 mg lost an average of 14.9% of body weight over 68 weeks, compared with 2.4% on placebo. About half lost 15% or more. People with type 2 diabetes lost less: 9.6% on the same dose in STEP 2.

How much weight do people lose on Zepbound or Mounjaro?

Both are tirzepatide. In SURMOUNT-1, adults without diabetes lost an average of 15.0%, 19.5% and 20.9% of body weight over 72 weeks on the 5, 10 and 15 mg doses, compared with 3.1% on placebo.

How much weight do people lose on Ozempic?

Ozempic is semaglutide at diabetes doses up to 2 mg. The closest trial figure is the 1 mg arm of STEP 2, in adults with type 2 diabetes, who lost an average of 7.0% over 68 weeks versus 3.4% on placebo.

How much weight do people lose on orforglipron (Foundayo)?

Orforglipron, sold as Foundayo, is a once-daily GLP-1 pill. In the ATTAIN-1 trial, adults with obesity and without diabetes lost an average of 11.2% of body weight over 72 weeks on 36 mg, 8.4% on 12 mg and 7.5% on 6 mg, against 2.1% on placebo.9

How much of GLP-1 weight loss is muscle?

Reviews of the trial data put lean mass at anywhere from about 15% to 40 to 60% of the weight lost, depending on the study. Protein of about 1.6 g/kg a day and regular resistance training are the two levers with evidence for moving it toward the low end. The full answer is here.
