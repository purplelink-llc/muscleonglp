Ben Ampel, PhD

Who edits this site

Ben Ampel is an assistant professor of computer information systems at Georgia State University's J. Mack Robinson College of Business, where he directs the Center for CyberAI Research. He earned his PhD at the University of Arizona. His research builds and evaluates AI systems, which is the same discipline this site applies to a different question: what the published evidence actually says, and how sure anyone can be.

He edits every article, calculator and guide on MuscleOnGLP. His academic profile, publications and CV are at bampel.com.

How the evidence is checked

Every factual claim on this site links to a specific source, and the sources follow a fixed order of preference (systematic reviews and meta-analyses first, then randomized trials, then narrative reviews and official labeling). Figures are checked against the source before publishing. Where the evidence comes from people who were not taking a GLP-1, or from a preprint, the text says so. The full process, including how AI-assisted drafting is used and reviewed, is in the editorial policy.
