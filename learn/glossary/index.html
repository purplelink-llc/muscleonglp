GLP-1 and muscle glossary

23 terms you will meet in GLP-1 muscle advice, each in a sentence or two, with a link to the page that explains it properly and cites the research.

B C D F G L P R S T W
Bioimpedance (BIA)
A way of estimating body composition by passing a small electrical current through the body, used by InBody machines and smart scales. Readings shift with hydration, so they are best read as a trend under the same conditions. DEXA vs InBody on a GLP-1 →
BMR (basal metabolic rate)
The calories your body burns at complete rest. Calculators estimate it from weight, height, age and sex, commonly with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Calorie, protein and macro calculator →
Calorie deficit
Eating fewer calories than you burn in a day (your TDEE). A GLP-1 creates one mostly by reducing appetite; the size of the deficit sets the pace of weight loss. Calorie, protein and macro calculator →
Complete protein
A protein that supplies all the essential amino acids the body cannot make. Whey, milk, eggs, meat, fish and soy are complete; most single plant proteins are short on one, which a blend can cover. Whey, plant or collagen →
Creatine
A compound the body makes and stores in muscle, where it recharges energy for short, hard efforts. As a supplement it makes training slightly more productive. It raises the creatinine marker on blood tests without harming the kidneys in healthy people. Creatine and GLP-1s →
DEXA (DXA) scan
Dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry: a low-dose scan that measures fat, lean mass and bone separately. It is the method most GLP-1 body-composition trials use. DEXA vs InBody on a GLP-1 →
Dose escalation
Stepping a GLP-1 up from a starting dose to a maintenance dose. Labeled schedules for semaglutide and tirzepatide hold each dose for at least four weeks; side effects cluster around the increases. GLP-1 dose schedules, side by side →
Five-times chair stand
A strength test: the time to stand up from a chair and sit back down five times, arms crossed. The European sarcopenia consensus uses more than 15 seconds as a sign of low strength. Muscle strength check →
GLP-1 receptor agonist
A medication that mimics the gut hormone GLP-1, slowing stomach emptying and reducing appetite. Semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy) is one; tirzepatide adds a second hormone target. Does Ozempic cause muscle loss? →
Grip strength
How hard you can squeeze a hand dynamometer, a quick proxy for overall muscle strength. The European sarcopenia consensus treats below 27 kg in men or 16 kg in women as low. Muscle strength check →
Lean mass (lean body mass)
Everything in the body that is not fat: muscle, bone, organs and water. Depending on the study, about 15 percent to 40 to 60 percent of the weight lost on a GLP-1 is lean mass. Lean body mass calculator →
Leucine threshold
The amount of the amino acid leucine, about 2.5 to 3 g, that a meal seems to need to switch on muscle building fully. It works out to roughly 25 to 30 g of high-quality protein. Protein timing on a GLP-1 →
PDCAAS
The protein digestibility-corrected amino acid score, the FAO/WHO method for rating protein quality. Scores are capped at 1.0, so it cannot rank proteins that both reach the cap. Protein without meat →
Progressive overload
Gradually asking more of a muscle over time, by adding weight, repetitions or difficulty. It is what keeps resistance training effective after the first weeks. Beginner resistance training on a GLP-1 →
Protein target (g/kg)
Daily protein expressed in grams per kilogram of body weight. A four-society GLP-1 advisory uses 1.2 to 1.6 g/kg during weight loss. How much protein you need on a GLP-1 →
Reps in reserve (RIR)
How many more repetitions you could have done when you stop a set. Stopping with two or three in reserve is enough to build muscle and safer while learning technique. Beginner resistance training on a GLP-1 →
Resistance training
Exercise that makes muscles work against a load: weights, machines, bands or bodyweight. With enough protein, it is the main lever for keeping muscle during weight loss. The best exercises to preserve muscle →
Retatrutide
An investigational medication that acts on three hormone receptors (GIP, GLP-1 and glucagon). It is not approved, and its lean-mass data are still limited. Retatrutide and muscle →
Sarcopenia
Loss of muscle strength together with low muscle quantity or quality. The European consensus (EWGSOP2) puts low strength first in the diagnosis. 5 at-home ways to tell if you're losing muscle →
TDEE (total daily energy expenditure)
All the calories you burn in a day: your BMR scaled up by an activity factor. Eating below it is a calorie deficit. Calorie, protein and macro calculator →
Tirzepatide
A medication that acts on both the GIP and GLP-1 receptors, sold as Mounjaro and Zepbound. In the SURMOUNT-1 body-composition data about a quarter of the weight lost was lean mass. Ozempic vs Mounjaro vs Zepbound →
Weight regain after stopping
The return of lost weight after a GLP-1 is stopped. In the STEP 1 extension, people regained about two thirds of their lost weight within a year of stopping semaglutide. Muscle after stopping Ozempic →
Weight-loss plateau
A stretch when the scale stops moving even though you are still on the medication. Composition can keep improving during one, which is why strength is worth tracking. Plateaus and muscle →
