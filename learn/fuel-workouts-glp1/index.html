How to fuel workouts on a GLP-1 when you are barely hungry

Strength training is how you keep muscle on a GLP-1, but eating around it is hard when your appetite is gone. Here is what matters, what does not, and the one caution if you have diabetes.

Updated October 27, 2026 Evidence-based, every claim cited

Key takeaways

  • Total daily protein beats timing. A meta-analysis found protein timing had no significant effect on muscle growth once total protein was matched.1
  • A meal or shake with about 25 to 30 grams of protein after training is a practical way to reach your daily target.2
  • Before training, eat a small snack only if your stomach allows. Many people lift fine without one.
  • GI side effects can cause dehydration, which the Wegovy label links to kidney injury.3 Drink before and during sessions.
  • If you take insulin or a sulfonylurea for type 2 diabetes, plan exercise and food with your prescriber: the label warns of low blood sugar in that combination.3

Short answer. On a GLP-1, fuel the day rather than the workout. Hit your daily protein target, put about 25 to 30 grams of it in a meal or shake in the hours after training, and eat a small snack beforehand only if you can.1,2 Stay hydrated, and talk to your prescriber first if you take insulin or a sulfonylurea.3

Workout fueling advice is usually written for people who are hungry. On a GLP-1, the problem runs the other way. Nausea affected 43.9 percent of people on semaglutide 2.4 mg in the STEP 1 to 3 trials, against 16.1 percent on placebo.4 Therefore the useful question is which parts of fueling are worth the effort, and which you can skip.

What matters most: protein across the day

The research on protein timing is reassuring for anyone who cannot eat on a schedule. A meta-analysis of protein timing studies found no significant effect on muscle strength or growth once total daily protein intake was matched.1 The so-called anabolic window (the idea that protein must arrive within minutes of lifting) is not supported once the total is accounted for.

The daily total is where to spend your effort. A joint advisory from four medical societies suggests 1.2 to 1.6 g/kg of body weight a day during active GLP-1 weight loss.5 Splitting that into a few servings of about 25 to 30 grams helps, because that amount appears to maximize the muscle-building response to a single meal in most adults.2 The meal after training is a natural place for one of those servings.

Before training: small, optional, and tolerable

A snack before lifting can help some people feel steadier during a session. Size it to your stomach, not to a sports-nutrition chart. A yogurt, a small shake or a piece of toast with cheese one to three hours beforehand is plenty. If food before training makes you nauseous, skip it. A short strength session does not depend on a pre-workout meal, and your daily protein total does the heavier lifting.1

Fluids are the part not to skip

Vomiting and diarrhea are among the common GI side effects of these drugs.4 The Wegovy label warns that dehydration from GI side effects has been linked to acute kidney injury.3 Drink before a session and between sets, and take extra care on days around a dose increase, when GI symptoms cluster.4 If you cannot keep fluids down, call your prescriber rather than training.

The low blood sugar caution

GLP-1 drugs alone rarely cause low blood sugar.3 However, the Wegovy label warns of a higher risk of hypoglycemia (low blood sugar) in people with type 2 diabetes who also take insulin or an insulin secretagogue such as a sulfonylurea.3 Exercise lowers blood sugar too. If that combination describes you, plan workout timing, food and any medication adjustments with your prescriber before starting a training program.

A simple training-day template

Use this as a starting point and adjust it to your appetite:

  • Earlier in the day: one protein serving of about 25 to 30 grams, if you can manage it.2
  • One to three hours before: a small optional snack, plus a glass of water.
  • During: water between sets.
  • After: a meal or shake with about 25 to 30 grams of protein. A shake that passes the label checklist works when food does not appeal.
  • The rest of the day: enough protein to reach your daily target. The calorie and protein calculator gives you the number.

Frequently asked

What should I eat before a workout on a GLP-1?

If you can manage it, a small snack with some protein and some carbohydrate one to three hours before training, sized to what your stomach tolerates. The bigger lever is total protein for the day: protein timing had no significant effect on muscle growth once total daily protein was matched.

Do I need protein right after lifting on Ozempic?

Not within a narrow window. A meal or shake with about 25 to 30 grams of protein in the hours after training is a practical way to reach your daily target.

Can I work out on an empty stomach on a GLP-1?

Many people can, especially for short strength sessions. The exception is people with type 2 diabetes who also take insulin or a sulfonylurea, who should plan exercise timing and food with their prescriber.
