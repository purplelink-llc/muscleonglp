For clinicians, dietitians and coaches

If you work with people on semaglutide or tirzepatide, everything free on this site is yours to use: link it, print it, hand it to a patient or client, and cite it.

Tools you can hand to a patient

  • Calorie, protein and macro calculator: Mifflin-St Jeor energy needs, a 1.6 to 2.2 g/kg protein target beside the 1.2 to 1.6 g/kg GLP-1 advisory range, grams per meal, and fat, carbohydrate and fiber at the calorie target.
  • Protein food calculator: a patient taps the foods they eat and sees the day add up against their target, with USDA values for 23 common foods.
  • Lean body mass calculator: Boer and Hume estimates of lean mass, and the share of it a GLP-1 loss could take with and without a plan.
  • Muscle strength check: grip strength and the five-times chair stand against the EWGSOP2 cut-offs, grip centiles for age and sex (Dodds 2014), a built-in chair-stand timer, and the change since a previous reading. Useful for a monthly check between visits.
  • Muscle loss calculator: the lean-mass share of the loss with and without protein and resistance training, from published ranges.
  • Weight loss calculator: STEP and SURMOUNT trial averages applied to the patient's own weight.
  • Weight-regain calculator: what the withdrawal trials found after stopping, and where muscle changes the outcome.

Every calculator has two buttons under the inputs. Copy link to these results makes a link that reopens with the same numbers, so you can fill it in during a visit and send it. Print for your appointment produces a clean printout of the inputs and results without ads or sign-up forms. Nothing a visitor types leaves their browser.

Figures you can cite

The GLP-1 muscle loss statistics page collects the lean-mass, regain and adherence figures with a primary source beside each one, and gives a ready-made citation. The evidence library lists every study the site relies on, with links to the papers. Please cite the primary study where you can; cite this site for the synthesis.

A citation in this form works: MuscleOnGLP. GLP-1 muscle loss statistics. getmuscleonglp.com/learn/glp1-muscle-loss-statistics/. Accessed [date].

Linking to the site

Patient handouts, clinic resource pages, course reading lists and community wikis may link to any page without asking. A plain text link is best, for example GLP-1 protein calculator or does Ozempic cause muscle loss? There is nothing to pay and no permission to request.

What the site does not do

  • No dosing, titration or sourcing advice. The split-dose calculator only does arithmetic on numbers a prescriber has already set.
  • No manufacturer funding. The site is not affiliated with Novo Nordisk, Eli Lilly or any medication manufacturer. It is paid for by guide sales, display ads and labelled Amazon links, as the editorial policy sets out.
  • No individual predictions. The calculators apply population ranges. They say so on every result.

Corrections and suggestions

If a figure is wrong, out of date or missing a better source, write to the address on the contact page with the page and the source. Verified errors are fixed promptly, and a correction that changes a recommendation is noted on the page rather than edited away. Suggestions for a tool or a topic your patients ask about are welcome the same way.

This site is educational and does not constitute medical advice. It gives no medical, dosing or sourcing advice. The split-dose calculator on this site only does arithmetic on the prescription numbers you enter, so confirm any change with your prescriber or pharmacist.
