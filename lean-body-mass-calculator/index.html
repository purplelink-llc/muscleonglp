Lean body mass calculator

Your estimated lean mass from height and weight, and how much of it a GLP-1 weight loss could take, with and without enough protein and resistance training.

Short answer. Lean body mass is everything that is not fat: muscle, bone, organs and water. Two standard equations estimate it from height, weight and sex.1,2 On a GLP-1, anywhere from about 15 percent to 40 to 60 percent of the weight lost is lean mass, depending on the study, so the size of the loss and the plan behind it decide how much of your lean mass goes with it.3

How this is calculated

Lean body mass. The calculator averages two published equations and shows both as a range. Boer (1984): for men, 0.407 × weight(kg) + 0.267 × height(cm) − 19.2; for women, 0.252 × weight + 0.473 × height − 48.3.1 Hume (1966): for men, 0.32810 × weight + 0.33929 × height − 29.5336; for women, 0.29569 × weight + 0.41813 × height − 43.2933.2

The GLP-1 loss. Reviews of the trial data put lean mass at anywhere from about 15 percent to 40 to 60 percent of the weight lost on a GLP-1, depending on the study, and the SURMOUNT-1 body-composition substudy measured about a quarter for tirzepatide.3 The "without" figure uses the model's high end, 40 percent, scaled for the medication; some studies report more. The "with a plan" range applies a resistance-training effect measured in older adults dieting without a GLP-1 (an extrapolation), up to the low end reported in GLP-1 trials.4 These are the same numbers the muscle loss calculator uses.

Honest limits

Three limits apply. First, both equations were developed largely in people without obesity, and estimates at a high body mass index are less reliable. Second, lean mass includes water, organs and bone, so not all lean loss is muscle. Third, the GLP-1 shares are population averages from trials, not a prediction for one person. If the number matters to a decision, a DEXA or InBody scan measures it.

Frequently asked

How do you calculate lean body mass?

Without a scan, lean body mass is estimated from height, weight and sex with published equations. This calculator averages two of them, Boer (1984) and Hume (1966), and shows both. A DEXA scan measures it directly.

How much lean mass do people lose on a GLP-1?

Reviews of the trial data put lean mass at anywhere from about 15 percent to 40 to 60 percent of the weight lost on a GLP-1, depending on the study, and about a quarter in the SURMOUNT-1 tirzepatide substudy. Protein and resistance training are the two levers with evidence for moving it toward the low end.

Is the lean body mass estimate accurate if I have obesity?

Less so. Height-and-weight equations were built mostly in people without obesity and can misjudge lean mass at a high body mass index. Treat the number as a starting estimate and use a DEXA or bioimpedance scan if you need a measurement.
