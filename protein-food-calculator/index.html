How the target is set

The target is your weight in kilograms times 1.2 at the low end and 1.6 at the high end. That is the range the joint advisory from four nutrition and obesity societies gives for adults losing weight on a GLP-1.1 Strength-training studies find the benefit of extra protein levels off near 1.6 g/kg, so the high end is where more stops helping for most people.4 If you carry a lot of fat mass, a target scaled to total weight can run high; the calorie, protein and macro calculator shows both ranges side by side.

Spread across the day matters too. About 0.4 g/kg per meal over four meals is a practical way to reach the total without one large plate.5 People with kidney disease should ask a clinician before raising protein.

Frequently asked

How much protein do I need on a GLP-1?

A four-society joint advisory on nutrition during GLP-1 therapy puts the target at about 1.2 to 1.6 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight a day during active weight loss. For a 90 kg (198 lb) person that is roughly 108 to 144 grams. People with kidney disease should ask a clinician before raising protein.

Which foods have the most protein per serving?

Per 100 g cooked, chicken breast has about 31 g, lean ground beef 26 g, and salmon, canned tuna and shrimp 24 to 25 g. A cup of low-fat cottage cheese has about 28 g and a scoop of whey about 24 g. Values are from USDA FoodData Central and vary by brand and preparation.

How do I hit a protein target with a small appetite?

Split the target over three to five small meals, eat the protein first, and use soft or liquid sources (Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, milk, a whey shake) on low-appetite days. The builder above shows how quickly those add up.

How much protein is in an egg?

A large egg has about 6 g of protein, according to USDA FoodData Central. Three eggs come to about 18 g, which is why eggs work better as one part of a meal than as the whole protein source.

Practical gear to hit your target

Optional tools that make the number easier to reach. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

  • Unflavored whey isolate stirs into yogurt, oats or soup for about 24 g a scoop without a sweet shake.
  • A shaker bottle makes a fast serving on the days solid food will not go down.
